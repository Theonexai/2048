How to play

Use the arrow keys or swipe to move every tile. Matching numbers merge into one and add to your score. A new 2 or 4 appears after each move. Reach 2048 to win; keep playing if you want to go further.

Inspired by 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli, based on 1024 by Veewo Studio and conceptually similar to Threes by Asher Vollmer.
